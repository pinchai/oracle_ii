
# SQL Exercise:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Level 1 --- Basic Queries (1--10)

1.  Show all employees' first name, last name, and salary.
2.  Show employees whose salary is greater than 5000.
3.  Show employees hired after 2005.
4.  Display distinct department IDs from EMPLOYEES.
5.  Show employees whose commission_pct is NULL.
6.  Show employees working in department 50.
7.  Show employees whose last name starts with 'S'.
8.  Display employees ordered by salary descending.
9.  Show the top 5 highest paid employees.
10. Display employee email and phone number.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Level 2 --- Aggregate Functions (11--20)

11. Find the average salary of all employees.
12. Find the maximum salary.
13. Find the minimum salary.
14. Count the total number of employees.
15. Find the number of employees per department.
16. Find the average salary per department.
17. Find departments with more than 5 employees.
18. Find the total salary paid per department.
19. Find the highest salary per job_id.
20. Find the lowest salary per job_id.
